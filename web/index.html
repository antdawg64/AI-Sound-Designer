    From silent clip to finished sound in three steps

    No sound library to dig through and no software to learn.

    1

    Upload your video

    Pick a short clip and tell us the feeling you want for the music, if you have one in mind.

    2

    The AI does the sound design

    It studies every shot, places effects on the moments that need them and scores original music. It takes a few minutes.

    3

    Fine-tune and download

    Adjust anything on the timeline, then download the finished video, plus the music and effects on their own.

    Everything your video needs to be heard

    One upload gives you a full soundtrack, already mixed.

    Synced sound effects

    Footsteps, impacts, motors and room sound, timed to what is on screen.

    Original music

    Composed for your clip, following its mood from the first shot to the last.

    A balanced mix

    Effects and music are levelled together, and kept under any voice in your video.

    Separate files

    Download the music and the effects as their own files for your editing app.
